Writing an opinion essay

When you write an opinion essay, you must say what you think about a topic and try to convince the reader

of your point of view on that topic. To do that, you should first  introduce the topic and state your opinion.

Then, you should give the reasons that support your view, and finally, you should write a conclusion where

you

summarise

your

arguments

and

repeat

your

opinion

using

different  words.
