QUESTION 1 (17 p.)

a)  With information from the text, write a definition of “immersive fallacy” as  conceived by

Salen and Zimmerman, cited in this article. (5 p.)

Immersive fallacy is the (common) misconception / the wrong idea that video

players  believe  they  are  part  of  the  imaginary  world  of  the  game,  that  they

reach a state where they forget  they are playing a game (they believe the game

is reality).

b)  What do these two authors, Salen and Zimmerman, argue instead? (4 p.)

They  argue  that  the  players  know  (are  aware)  that  the  play  situation  is  an

artificial situation and that is precisely why they enjoy these games. The games

are entertaining precisely because of their artificiality.

c)  Reread  the  first  paragraphs  of  the  article.  Is  Salen  and  Zimmerman’s  point  of  view

supported  or  contradicted  by  the  students’  point  of  view  about  “being  immersed”

/”immersion”? Account for your answer. (4 p.)

Supported, because the students said that an important part of immersion for

them was that it generated a sense of “escapism and freedom from reality”. /

because they thought that when you are immersed in a game you feel free and

escape from reality.
