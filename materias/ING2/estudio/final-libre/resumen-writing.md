# Writing — resumen para el final

## Claves

1. **Identificá el tipo en la consigna** (ver tabla). Equivocarte de tipo es el error más caro.
2. **Usá las ideas del texto, parafraseadas.** No inventes argumentos complejos y no copies
   oraciones enteras. Es lo que se premia.
3. **4 párrafos, ~250 palabras** (entre 220 y 280). Cada párrafo arranca con una oración que
   dice de qué trata.
4. **Una idea + un ejemplo o dato** por oración o par de oraciones.
5. **Un conector en cada cambio de idea** (*To begin with, In addition, However, As a result*).
6. **Registro formal:** sin contracciones (*do not*, no *don't*), sin *very / a lot of / good*.
7. **Tiempo sugerido:** 5 min para planear, 30 para escribir y 5 para revisar. En la revisión
   buscá tus errores típicos: *lose/loose*, *their/they*, la *-s* de tercera persona (*it proposes*).

## Cómo reconocer el tipo

| Si la consigna dice… | Tipo |
|---|---|
| *balanced argument, advantages and disadvantages, discuss both views* | **For and against** |
| *Do you agree or disagree? What is your opinion? To what extent…?* | **Opinion** |
| *problems … and suggest solutions, what can be done about…* | **Problems and solutions** |

## Formatos

| ¶ | For and against | Opinion | Problems and solutions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tema y contexto, **sin opinión** | Tema + **tu opinión** | Presentar el problema |
| 2 | Ventajas, con ejemplos | Razón 1, con ejemplos | Problemas: causas y efectos |
| 3 | Desventajas, con ejemplos | Razón 2, con ejemplos | Soluciones, cada una con su resultado |
| 4 | Resumen + **recién acá tu opinión** | Repetís la opinión con otras palabras | Conclusión: qué pasa si se aplican |
| ⚠ | Nada de *I think* antes del ¶4 | **Un solo lado:** no menciones el otro, te lo tachan | Cada solución responde a un problema que nombraste |

## Expresiones

### Introducción (sirven para los tres tipos)

| Para… | Expresión |
|---|---|
| Presentar el tema | *In recent years, X has become…* / *Nowadays, X is one of the most debated issues in…* |
| Dar contexto | *Since the pandemic, …* / *This has become increasingly common as…* |
| Abrir con pregunta | *Is it true that X will change the way we work?* |
| Anunciar el enfoque | *This essay will examine…* (for and against) / *I strongly believe that…* (opinion) / *However, it also creates several problems.* (problems and solutions) |

### Cuerpo, según el tipo

| Tipo | Expresiones |
|---|---|
| **For and against** | *On the one hand, …* / *The main advantage of X is…* / *On the other hand, …* / *One major drawback is…* / *Critics argue that…* |
| **Opinion** | *The main reason why I agree is that…* / *Another reason is that…* / *A clear example of this is…* |
| **Problems and solutions** | *One of the main problems is…* / *Another problem is that…* / *This leads to…* / *One possible solution would be to…* / *Another measure could be…* / *This would result in…* |

### Conectores

| Función | Conectores |
|---|---|
| Ordenar | *To begin with, … / Secondly, … / Finally, … / Last but not least, …* |
| Sumar | *In addition, … / Moreover, … / Furthermore, … / What is more, …* |
| Contrastar | *However, … / On the other hand, … / Although + oración / Despite + sustantivo o -ing / whereas* |
| Causa | *because + oración / due to + sustantivo / since + oración* |
| Efecto | *As a result, … / Therefore, … / This leads to… / which leads to…* |
| Ejemplo | *For example, … / For instance, … / such as + sustantivo* |
| Finalidad | *in order to + verbo / so that + oración* |

### Conclusión

| Tipo | Expresiones |
|---|---|
| For and against | *Taking everything into account, I believe that…* / *On balance, although…, I feel that…* |
| Opinion | *For these reasons, I am convinced that…* / *To conclude, I firmly believe that…* |
| Problems and solutions | *To sum up, X affects… However, if these measures are taken, …* / *In conclusion, although X causes…, it can be solved if…* |

### Revisión final (tus errores típicos)

| Error | Correcto | Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| *This have* | *This has* | 3.ª persona: *has / is / -s* |
| *an hybrid* | *a hybrid* | *an* solo antes de sonido vocal |
| *a software* | *software* | incontable: *software, information, advice, work* |
| *affects directly on* | *directly affects* | *affect* no lleva preposición |
| *journals* | *working days* | falso amigo: jornada |
| *work weather* | *work environment* | *weather* = clima meteorológico |
| *loose* | *lose* | *lose* = perder; *loose* = flojo |

## Ejemplos

### For and against — *"Online learning at university: advantages and disadvantages"*

In recent years, more and more universities have started to offer courses online. This
has opened a debate among students and lecturers about whether online learning is as
effective as traditional classes.

On the one hand, online learning has several advantages. To begin with, students can
study at their own pace and watch recorded classes as many times as they need. In
addition, it saves time and money, since students do not have to travel to the university
every day. This is particularly important for those who work or live far from the campus.

On the other hand, online learning also has some drawbacks. One major concern is that
students may feel isolated, because they have fewer opportunities to interact with their
classmates and teachers. Moreover, not everyone has a reliable internet connection or a
quiet place to study at home, which may result in lower performance.

Taking everything into account, I believe that online learning is a useful tool, but it
should not completely replace face-to-face classes. In my view, a combination of both is
the best option, as it offers flexibility without losing the contact between students and
teachers.

*Por qué funciona:* el ¶1 no opina, cada cuerpo tiene dos ideas con su ejemplo y la opinión
aparece recién en el ¶4.

### Opinion — *"Smartphones should be banned in secondary schools. Do you agree?"*

Smartphones have become part of teenagers' everyday lives, and many schools are now
discussing whether they should be allowed in the classroom. I strongly believe that they
should be banned during school hours.

The main reason why I agree is that smartphones distract students. Notifications from
social media and games constantly interrupt their concentration, and as a result, they
find it more difficult to follow the lessons. For example, teachers often report that
students pay more attention when phones are kept out of the classroom.

Another reason is that a ban improves relationships between students. Without phones,
they spend their breaks talking and playing with their classmates instead of looking at a
screen. Furthermore, it reduces problems such as cyberbullying during school hours.

For these reasons, I am convinced that banning smartphones in secondary schools is the
right decision. Students would concentrate better and interact more with each other, which
would make school a better place to learn.

*Por qué funciona:* la opinión está en la primera y en la última oración, y **en ningún
momento** se menciona algo a favor de los celulares.

### Problems and solutions — *"Traffic congestion in big cities: problems and solutions"*

Traffic congestion has become one of the most serious problems in big cities around the
world. Every day, millions of people spend hours in their cars or on crowded buses.

One of the main problems is the time people lose. Long journeys to work and school cause
stress and reduce productivity. In addition, thousands of cars stuck in traffic produce
large amounts of pollution, which leads to health problems such as asthma.

One possible solution would be to improve public transport. If buses and trains were
faster and more frequent, more people would leave their cars at home. Another measure
could be to build safe cycle lanes, which would encourage people to cycle for short
journeys. Finally, companies could allow employees to work from home some days a week,
which would reduce the number of commuters.

To sum up, traffic congestion affects both our time and our health. However, if
governments and companies take these measures, cities could become cleaner and much
easier to move around.

*Por qué funciona:* cada solución ataca un problema nombrado en el ¶2 (menos autos → menos
tiempo perdido y menos contaminación) y dice qué resultado tendría (*would…*).
