**Esquema posible:**
1. *Intro:* AI tools are now part of everyday university life; debate between efficiency and learning.
2. *For:* immediate feedback and explanations (personal tutor); saves time on repetitive tasks;
   engineers will use AI at work, so they must learn to use it critically.
3. *Against:* cognitive offloading (students may never develop problem-solving skills; Danish study);
   inaccurate information; unfair assessment if some students rely on AI.
4. *Conclusion + opinion:* a help **if** assessment changes (process visible: oral exams, explaining
   decisions).

Sumá evidencia del reading del mismo examen: el estudio danés y el ejemplo de los
exámenes orales.

**Modelo (~250 palabras, propio):**

> Artificial intelligence has entered engineering faculties faster than any previous
> technology. While some lecturers see it as a powerful learning tool, others fear that
> it may prevent students from developing essential skills.
>
> There are several arguments in favour of AI in education. To begin with, chatbots can
> act as personal tutors, providing immediate explanations at any time of the day.
> Furthermore, they reduce the time spent on repetitive tasks, such as formatting reports
> or searching for documentation. Finally, since engineers will work with AI tools
> throughout their careers, university is the right place to learn to use them critically.
>
> On the other hand, the risks are considerable. The main concern is cognitive
> offloading: a student who lets a chatbot solve every exercise may pass the course
> without learning to reason. A study at a Danish university showed that students with
> restricted access to AI scored 17 per cent higher in an unassisted exam. In addition,
> these tools sometimes produce inaccurate information, which students without solid
> knowledge cannot detect.
>
> All things considered, I believe that AI is a help rather than a threat, provided that
> universities adapt their assessment methods. If students are asked to explain their
> reasoning, for instance in oral exams, copying becomes pointless and the machine
> becomes a tutor instead of a shortcut.

## ORAL

Argumentos posibles para la moción *"students should be allowed to use AI tools in all
university assessments"*:
