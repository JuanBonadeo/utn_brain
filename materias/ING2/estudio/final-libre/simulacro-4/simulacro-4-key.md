## PART 2 — WRITING (40 p.)

> **Adaptación del tema:** la consigna pide un *problems and solutions essay* sobre el
> trabajo remoto de ingenieros y desarrolladores (el enunciado "more good than harm" queda
> como contexto del seminario), porque el tipo de ensayo de este simulacro no es de opinión.

**Grilla orientativa (propia; la cátedra no publica la suya):**

| Criterio | Puntos |
|---|---|
| Cumple la consigna (problems and solutions): ¶1 presenta el problema; ¶2 problemas/causas y efectos; ¶3 soluciones, cada una con su resultado esperado; ¶4 conclusión | 10 |
| Organización: 4 párrafos, *topic sentences*, conectores | 10 |
| Gramática: tiempos, concordancia, condicionales y modales (*would*, *could*) bien usados | 10 |
| Vocabulario académico, sin repetir, con nominalización | 10 |

> **Usá las ideas del texto parafraseadas; no inventes argumentos propios complejos ni copies
> oraciones enteras.** El texto trae cuatro problemas con su solución: (1) aislamiento →
> días presenciales y pausas de café virtuales; (2) límites difusos entre trabajo y vida
> privada, burnout → horarios fijos y derecho a desconectarse; (3) los juniors aprenden más
> lento → mentor y *pair programming* por videollamada; (4) mala comunicación por el chat →
> decisiones escritas en documentos compartidos.
> Frases útiles: *One of the main problems is…*, *This leads to…*, *One possible solution
> would be…*, *As a result…*.

**Esquema posible:**
1. *Intro:* remote work is normal for engineers/developers; flexibility, but also problems.
2. *Problems + effects:* isolation → lower motivation; blurred boundaries → burnout; juniors
   learn slowly; chat decisions get lost → misunderstandings.
3. *Solutions + results:* in-person days → team feeling; fixed hours → less burnout; mentor →
   faster learning; shared documents → less confusion.
4. *Conclusion:* not a problem in itself, but it needs careful management.

**Modelo (~250 palabras, propio):**

> Remote work has become a normal way of working for engineers and software developers.
> Although it brings flexibility, it also creates problems that companies cannot ignore.
> Since many firms have kept this model after the pandemic, it is important to understand its risks.
>
> One of the main problems is isolation. Developers who work alone at home may lose contact
> with their colleagues, which leads to lower motivation. In addition, work and private life
> become hard to separate: without a commute, many people start earlier and finish later, and
> this leads to burnout. Junior engineers also suffer, because they miss the informal advice
> that they would receive in an office, and as a result they learn more slowly. Finally,
> decisions taken in chat messages are difficult to find later, which causes
> misunderstandings.
>
> Fortunately, each of these problems has a possible solution. Companies could organise
> regular in-person days and virtual coffee breaks; as a result, workers would feel part of a
> team again. Fixed working hours and a right to disconnect would reduce the risk of burnout.
> Assigning a mentor to every newcomer, and encouraging pair programming by video call, would
> help juniors to learn faster. Moreover, recording important decisions in shared documents
> would make information easy to find and, consequently, reduce confusion.
>
> In conclusion, remote work is not a problem in itself, but it requires careful management.
> With these measures, engineers could keep its advantages while avoiding its main risks.
> Companies that act now will keep their teams motivated, healthy and productive.

## ORAL

Argumentos posibles para la moción *"engineering companies should require employees to work
from the office at least three days a week"* (for/against):

| For | Against |
|---|---|
| Juniors learn faster from informal advice and pair work. | Commuting wastes time and money, and many developers work better at home. |
| Teams build trust and spontaneous collaboration. | Companies lose talent in other cities or countries that want flexibility. |
| It separates work from home life and reduces isolation. | Productivity depends on results, not on presence; offices cost money. |

Si el examinador prefiere formato *problem/solution*: *Problem 1:* isolation of remote
developers → *solution:* a fixed in-person day per week → *result:* stronger teams without
losing flexibility. *Problem 2:* juniors learn slowly → *solution:* mentors and pair
programming → *result:* faster learning, in the office or online.
