**Question 2 (3 × 4 p.)**

a) Because cities will have to feed, move and protect very large populations (almost 70 %
of the world's population by 2050), and collecting information in real time through
sensors, cameras and connected devices allows them to manage urban services more
efficiently.

b) Because the same data that is collected to provide services can be used to watch
people: traffic cameras can recognise faces, and transport card data reveals where people
live, work and spend their evenings. Once that information exists, it can be used by
governments, companies or criminals for other purposes.

c) The author means that trust cannot be created with technology alone. Unlike a
sensor, it has to be earned, for example through transparent rules such as anonymising
data and publishing registers of sensors and algorithms.

**Question 3 — Connectors (4 × 3 p.)**

a) Sensors buried under parking spaces tell drivers, through a mobile app, where a free
space is available, **which** reduces traffic and emissions.

b) **Although** the project promised heated pavements and robotic deliveries, it was
abandoned in 2020.
*(También vale: **Despite** promising heated pavements and robotic deliveries, the
project was abandoned in 2020. Ojo: después de *despite* va **-ing** o un sustantivo,
nunca una oración con sujeto.)*

c) Some cities now anonymise the data collected in public spaces **in order to** ensure
that it cannot be linked to specific individuals.

d) Technology can make cities cleaner, safer and more efficient, **but** it needs the
trust of citizens.

Sobran: **unless** y **despite** (si no la usaste en la b). Corrección: 1 p. por elegir
bien el conector y 2 p. por la oración bien reescrita. *This* y *They* se eliminan al unir
(a y c), y en la c el verbo pasa a infinitivo.

**Question 4 — Cloze (10 × 1,2 p.)**

    1) due to      2) where      3) However     4) such as     5) in
    6) compared    7) Nevertheless   8) Despite   9) which    10) to

Sobran: **unless** y **although**.

- 3 y 7 son intercambiables: los dos conectores de contraste encajan en ambos huecos.
- 5: *an approach **in which** data is processed* (preposición + *which*).
- 8: *Despite these limitations* (sustantivo después de *despite*). *Although* no va
  porque la sigue un sustantivo, no una oración.
- 9: *which* y no *where*: el antecedente es *a hybrid architecture*, que es el sujeto de
  *combines*, no un lugar.

## PART 2 — WRITING (40 p.)

Grilla: la misma del simulacro 1 (consigna 10, organización 10, gramática 10, vocabulario 10).

**Esquema posible:**
1. *Intro:* automation is not new (Industrial Revolution); AI has reopened the debate.
2. *Optimists:* historically, technology created new jobs (programmers, data scientists,
   technicians); productivity → economic growth → demand; dangerous and repetitive tasks are automated.
3. *Pessimists:* the speed of change is unprecedented; it affects cognitive jobs, not only
   manual ones; new jobs require skills that displaced workers may not have.
4. *Opinion:* the result depends on education and retraining policies; engineers have a
   responsibility.

Frases que conviene usar: *"It is sometimes argued that…"*, *"Opponents of this view
claim that…"*, *"A striking example of this is…"*, *"On balance, I believe that…"*.
