### Unidad 1 — El examen final libre: formato, puntajes y estrategia

#### Conceptos clave
- **Libre = escrito + oral, ambos eliminatorios, cada uno con nota mínima 6.**
  Si no aprobás el escrito, no pasás al oral. La nota final es un entero; un
  promedio con ,50 se redondea hacia arriba (planificación 2026, sección 15).
- El programa de examen es el **programa analítico completo** (las dos unidades
  de la planificación: Unidad 1, *comprensión* de textos orales y escritos; Unidad 2,
  *producción* de textos orales y escritos), no lo que se haya visto en una comisión.
- Bibliografía obligatoria "punto 1": **Oxford EAP B1 completo**. El tema del oral
  puede salir de ahí o de los textos del escrito.
- Temática constante en todos los modelos: **tecnología y sus efectos** (video games,
  IA, robots, Python, energía, plásticos). Textos reales de divulgación (The
  Conversation, blogs de empresas, abstracts).

### Unidad 2 — Reading (Part 1-B): tipos de consigna

#### Conceptos clave
Cada tipo de consigna tiene una técnica. Las respuestas abiertas se corrigen por
contenido: una paráfrasis fiel del texto vale lo mismo que la frase literal.

| Consigna | Cómo resolverla |
|---|---|
| **Open questions** ("With information from the text…") | Ubicá el párrafo, parafraseá. Empezá repitiendo la estructura de la pregunta: *"Immersive fallacy is the misconception that…"* |
| **Supported or contradicted? Account for your answer** | Respondé **Supported / Contradicted** + *because* + cita o paráfrasis. |
| **Headings** (match headings to paragraphs) | Leé la primera y la última oración de cada párrafo. Siempre sobran 1–2 headings (distractores). |
| **Word references** ("What do the underlined words refer to?") | Buscá el antecedente **hacia atrás**: *them* → plural anterior, *this* → idea u oración previa, *such purposes* → el propósito recién nombrado. |
| **Definitions / synonyms** ("What expressions from the text can be replaced by…") | Vienen en orden y con la sección indicada. Tiene que ser **intercambiable**: misma categoría gramatical. |
| **Multiple choice "Write T"** | Descartá por lo que el texto *no dice*. Los distractores usan palabras del texto en otra relación. |
| **Flow-chart / diagrama** | Seguí la secuencia temporal del texto. "NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS **from the text**". |
| **List three tasks…** | Tres ítems distintos, cada uno con verbo + objeto. |
| **"What questions do these statements answer?"** (modelos viejos) | Armá la pregunta con auxiliar y *wh-word* correctos (ver Unidad 4). |

#### Ejercicios resueltos tipo

**1) Preguntas abiertas: final 14-11-2023** ("Why violence in video games isn't really a problem")

- *a) Definition of "immersive fallacy" (5 p.)* → "Immersive fallacy is the (common)
  misconception that video players believe they are part of the imaginary world of
  the game, that they reach a state where they forget they are playing a game."
- *b) What do Salen and Zimmerman argue instead? (4 p.)* → "They argue that players
  are aware that the play situation is artificial, and that is precisely why they
  enjoy these games."
- *c) Supported or contradicted by the students? (4 p.)* → "**Supported**, because the
  students said that an important part of immersion was that it generated a sense of
  'escapism and freedom from reality'."
- *d) Author's answer to the title (4 p.)* → "Games 'provide a frame for the
  imagination' while remaining 'firmly fictional', so players can tell fiction from
  reality."

Patrón: la KEY **reutiliza las palabras del texto** y agrega un conector de
explicación (*that is why, precisely because*).

**2) Word references y definiciones: parcial 1 2025** (texto de Rozum Robotics, KEY)

    such purposes (A)  → building complex (industrial-grade) applications
    This (C)           → using the open-source tooling
    them (C)           → servo-motors
    all the parts (E)  → robot control, payments, system monitoring, etc.

    working hard        → striving        win against → beat
    achievement         → milestone       free and open to anyone → open-source
    to join things together (noun) → glue

Ojo: en el repaso de un alumno figura *integration* para la última; la KEY dice
**glue** (la palabra del texto). La que vale es la de la KEY.

**3) Sinónimos intercambiables: parcial 2 (video games), KEY**

    bring up doubts → raise concerns      improve → enhance
    may rise        → can increase        promoting → fostering
    almost half     → nearly 48%          resulting in → leading to
    in contrast     → on the other hand   detachment → withdrawal
    excessive dependence → overreliance   anomalies → inconsistencies
    transforming    → reshaping           complex → multifaceted

Sirve también como banco de vocabulario académico para el writing.

**4) Headings: práctica "Robots at work"** (KEY)
A–5, B–7, C–9, D–4, E–1, F–8, G–3. Sobran 2 y 6 ("Preparing LGVs for takeover",
"Newspapers superseded by technology"): distractores que suenan plausibles pero
ningún párrafo los desarrolla.

### Unidad 3 — Listening y note-taking (Part 1-A)

#### Conceptos clave
- 15 p. en el final. Audios de tipo **charla académica o clase** (IELTS-like).
- Consignas: *Write ONLY ONE WORD/NUMBER*, *NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER*,
  T/F, asignar A/B/C, elegir el gráfico o la columna correcta.
- El límite de palabras es estricto: pasarse anula la respuesta.

#### Desarrollo: técnicas
- Antes del audio, **predecí la categoría** de cada hueco: "shaped like a ___" →
  sustantivo; "scientists from ___ countries" → número.
- Los números se dicen de muchas formas: *thirteen / 13*, *one / 1 more country*.
  La KEY acepta cualquiera de las dos.
- Note-taking (consigna de 20 p. en el parcial 2025): por sección, **ideas principales
  en frases cortas**, sin oraciones completas, solo sustantivos, verbos y números.
  Abreviá (→, +, w/, e.g.).
- Tips de la charla sobre note-taking del final 2023 (que además son el contenido
  evaluado): preparar con anticipación, un cuaderno por materia, fecha en la primera
  página, colores distintos, anotar solo las ideas clave (sustantivos, adjetivos,
  verbos), usar abreviaturas y símbolos, y revisar los apuntes después de clase
  comparándolos con otros.

#### Ejercicios resueltos tipo
**Práctica "The Robotic Float Project"** (audios en `fuentes/drive-libre/…/PRÁCTICA DE EXAMEN-PLAN 2023/`, KEY)

### Unidad 4 — Gramática y lenguaje académico

#### Conceptos clave
Gramática que aparece efectivamente en los exámenes y TPs de la cátedra:
1. **Tiempos verbales**: presente y pasado, simple y continuo (y presente perfecto).
2. **Voz pasiva** (el rephrase más frecuente).
3. **Conectores**: adición, contraste, causa/efecto, propósito, concesión.
4. **Lenguaje de comparación y contraste** (*like, unlike, similarly, compared to*).
5. **Hedging**: *it seems/appears that, may, could*.
6. **Nominalización** y sintagmas nominales complejos.
7. **Preguntas indirectas**.

#### Desarrollo

**1. Tiempos verbales: cloze del parcial 2025 (Guido van Rossum)**

    is · invented · was working · was looking · started · wanted ·
    was creating · took · named · lives · continues · doesn't lead* · supports · use

- *Past continuous* para la acción de fondo ("At that time, he **was working** at CWI"),
  *past simple* para la acción puntual que la interrumpe ("so he **started**…").
- *While he **was creating** it, he **took** ideas…*: *while* + continuo, y la principal en simple.
- *Today* + hábito o estado → presente simple (*lives, continues, supports, use*).
- (*) (12) "Although he ___ (not lead) the Python project anymore": el repaso de un alumno
  pone *is not leading*. Lo más natural es **doesn't lead** (estado permanente, mismo
  tiempo que *supports*). Es criterio mío: no hay KEY oficial de esta tarea.

**2. Rephrase (transformaciones): parcial 2, KEY**

    Playing video games may improve cognitive skills…
    → Cognitive skills may be improved by playing video games…          (pasiva con modal)

    Shigeru Miyamoto designed one of the most influential video games…
    → One of the most influential video games in history was designed by Shigeru Miyamoto.

    Apparently, players spend more and more time playing online games…
    → It seems (that) players spend more and more time playing online games.   (hedging)

    In the past, parents didn't allow children to play … because of concerns about health.
    → Due to health concerns, in the past parents didn't allow children to play … .

    Many experts are studying the effects video games have on social behaviour. (could)
    → Many experts are studying the effects video games could have on social behaviour.

Reglas:
- Pasiva = **be** (en el tiempo del verbo activo) + **participio**. Con modal: *modal + be + participio*.
  Pasado: *designed → was designed*. Presente continuo: *are studying → are being studied*.
- *Due to / because of* + **sustantivo** (*due to health concerns*); *because* + **oración**.
- La KEY oficial transcribe una versión con *because of* sobrante en la 4: es un error de
  tipeo. No repitas *because of* después de *due to*.

**3. Conectores**

| Función | Conectores | Nota de uso |
|---|---|---|
| Adición | *Furthermore, Moreover, In addition, What is more, Besides, Not only… but also* | a principio de oración, con coma |
| Contraste | *However, Nevertheless, On the other hand, On the contrary* | conectan dos oraciones |
| Concesión | *although / even though / while* + oración; *despite / in spite of* + sustantivo o -ing | *Despite **the** increase…*, *Although **AI usage increased**…* |
| Causa / efecto | *Consequently, Therefore, As a result, Thus, Hence*; *due to / because of* + sustantivo | |
| Propósito | *in order to / so as to* + infinitivo; *so that* + oración | |
| Ejemplo | *For instance, such as, e.g.* | |
| Aclaración | *In other words, That is to say* | |

TP "Rewriting" (Bettucci): reescribir la oración con el conector dado. Ejemplo resuelto (mío):

    Despite increased AI usage among students, they are not necessarily more comfortable with it. (ALTHOUGH)
    → Although AI usage among students has increased, they are not necessarily more comfortable with it.

    In addition to personalized learning, AI can assist educators by automating administrative tasks. (APART FROM)
    → Apart from personalizing learning, AI can assist educators by automating administrative tasks.

**4. Comparación y contraste: cloze del parcial 2025** (KEY)

    differ · such as · is · like · compared · Similarly · seem · unlike ·
    appears · same · However · Overall

- *languages **like** C++*: ejemplo; ***unlike** those*: contraste; ***Compared** to JavaScript*.
- *the **same** logic*; ***Overall**, most people agree…*: cierre que resume.

**5. Nominalización** (TP de la cátedra)
Pasar de verbo o adjetivo a sustantivo para sonar académico:
*achieve → achievement, assess → assessment, assume → assumption, define → definition,
evaluate → evaluation, examine → examination, investigate → investigation, measure →
measurement, occur → occurrence, predict → prediction, require → requirement, respond →
response, signify → significance, suggest → suggestion*;
*different → difference, strong → strength, deep → depth, clear → clarity,
difficult → difficulty, cautious → caution, violent → violence*.
En el writing: "*The **implementation** of AI…*" suena mejor que "*When we implement AI…*".

**6. Preguntas indirectas** (TP de la cátedra)
Frase introductoria + **orden afirmativo** (sin auxiliar *do*, sin invertir):

    How long is the bootcamp?        → Could you tell me how long the bootcamp is?
    Do you consider international applicants?
                                     → I'd like to know if/whether you consider international applicants.
    How can non-functional requirements be verified?
                                     → Could you explain how non-functional requirements can be verified?

Sirve en el oral para preguntarle al otro equipo o al moderador.

### Unidad 5 — Writing (Part 2): el ensayo argumentativo

#### Conceptos clave
- 40 p., **~250 palabras**. Consigna típica: *"write a balanced argument before presenting
  your own point of view. Points of view have to be accounted for by clear and convincing
  evidence."* Es un **for and against essay** con opinión al final.
- Temas tomados: *Beneficial and harmful effects of video games* (final 2023); *positive
  vs. negative effects of the growth of technology* (práctica). Practicados en el repaso:
  green hydrogen, IA en la investigación académica, autos autónomos.

#### Desarrollo: estructura de 4 párrafos

| Párrafo | Contenido | Regla |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Introducción | tema + contexto, **sin opinión** | gancho: pregunta retórica, contexto histórico o *general remark* |
| 2. Argumentos a favor | *topic sentence* + 2–3 razones con ejemplo o evidencia | sin *I think* |
| 3. Argumentos en contra | *topic sentence* + 2–3 razones con ejemplo o evidencia | sin *I think* |
| 4. Conclusión | resumen balanceado + **tu opinión** | recién acá *I believe…* |

Frases que conviene llevar memorizadas:
- **Intro:** *"X is one of the most debated issues of our time." / "The growing popularity
  of X has instigated a debate among…" / "Is it true that X will change the way we live?"*
- **A favor:** *"The main advantage of X is…" / "Advocates of X support the view that…" /
  "It is widely argued that…"*
- **En contra:** *"On the other hand, X does have its drawbacks." / "Opponents of X argue
  that…" / "A further common criticism of X is…"*
- **Conclusión:** *"All things considered, I believe that…" / "On balance, although there
  are some disadvantages, I feel that…" / "To sum up, it appears that the benefits of X
  outweigh the risks."*

Cómo sumar puntos: una idea por oración, **conectores** entre oraciones, nominalización,
hedging (*may, it appears that*) y **evidencia concreta** (un dato, un estudio, un ejemplo).
Los textos del reading del mismo examen sirven como fuente de evidencia: el final 2023
pidió el ensayo sobre video games con dos textos de video games en la Part 1-B.

#### Ejercicios resueltos tipo
**Modelo (propio, ~250 palabras) para la consigna del final 2023:**
*Beneficial and harmful effects of video games*

> The growing popularity of video games has instigated a debate among parents,
> researchers and policymakers. While some regard gaming as a waste of time, others
> claim that it can actually improve both body and mind.
>
> Advocates of video games point out several benefits. To begin with, games can develop
> physical and cognitive skills: a study of surgeons in Boston found that those who
> played video games were 27 per cent faster and made 37 per cent fewer errors.
> Furthermore, action games have been shown to speed up decision-making without reducing
> accuracy. In addition, some games, such as SPARX, have been used to reduce the symptoms
> of depression in young people.
>
> On the other hand, video games also have their drawbacks. Opponents argue that violent
> games may alter brain function after as little as a week of play, which could lead to
> more aggressive behaviour. Moreover, excessive gaming can result in social withdrawal
> and overreliance on screens, particularly among younger players.
>
> All things considered, I believe that the benefits of video games outweigh their risks,
> provided that they are played in moderation. As research suggests, players are aware
> that games are fictional, so the problem does not lie in the games themselves but in
> how much time is spent on them. Therefore, parents and educators should promote a
> balanced use rather than a ban.

Observá: la evidencia sale de los textos del mismo examen (*Video games are good for you!*
y el artículo de Lind), hay un conector en cada transición y la opinión aparece recién en
el último párrafo.

- Pendiente: un banco de 8–10 temas probables (IA, automatización, redes sociales,
  teletrabajo, energía, privacidad).

### Unidad 6 — Oral: el debate

#### Conceptos clave
- Grupal (2–3 alumnos), 20–30 min. Un docente modera y otro evalúa con rúbrica.
- Secuencia: (1) **presentación breve de la relevancia del tema** (3–5 min); (2) el
  moderador presenta la **moción** y asigna roles **a favor / en contra** (no elegís
  el lado); (3) debate moderado; (4) el tribunal delibera la nota individual.
- Tema: alguno de los textos del escrito o del libro obligatorio (Oxford EAP B1).

#### Desarrollo: expresiones útiles
- **Abrir:** *"To kick things off, it is widely argued that…" / "I would like to start by
  highlighting the fact that…"*
- **Acuerdo y desacuerdo, de más a menos:**
  *"I firmly believe your point is correct…"* → *"I concede your argument up to a point,
  but…"* → *"While there is some truth in what you say, …"* → *"I see your point, but I
  don't agree that…"* → *"I strongly disagree with that claim because…"*
- **Turnos:** *"If I may just add a point here…" / "Could I just say something about the
  previous point?" / "What is your stance on this?"*
- **Aclarar o ganar tiempo:** *"In other words, what I'm trying to say is…" / "That's an
  interesting question; let me clarify my position."*
- **Cerrar:** *"It appears we have reached a balance between our viewpoints regarding…"*

Preparación (sugerencia propia): para cada tema probable, armá 3 argumentos a favor y
3 en contra con un dato cada uno. Te pueden asignar cualquiera de los dos lados. Es el
mismo material que para el writing.

#### Dudas / pendientes
- El repaso enlaza un "Parcial oral debate – Inglés II.docx" en Google Docs que no está
  descargado.

### Unidad 7 — Vocabulario temático

#### Conceptos clave y desarrollo
**Números, gráficos y tendencias** (la planificación los incluye y el listening 2023 usó gráficos):

    99,2 → approximately / about / around one hundred
    19%  → almost twenty per cent      35% → more than a third
    24%  → nearly a quarter            22% → less than a quarter
    91%  → over ninety per cent        3/5 → three fifths   1/20 → one twentieth
    "Let's look at this chart" · "Here, you can see…" · "Notice this" · "This is my final chart"

Tendencias: *rise / increase / grow* ↔ *fall / decrease / drop*; *steadily, sharply,
slightly*; *peak, level off, remain stable*.

**Empresas:** *provide, revenue, based (in), employee, operate, specialize (in), subsidiary,
competitor, produce, exporter*.

**Trabajo y habilidades** (aviso "Hire Engineers", parcial 2022): *degree, qualities,
competency, common sense, dependability, interpersonal, trait, courage, organizational,
quantitative thinking, problem solving, curiosity, long-term*; *in charge of, responsible
for, deal with, specialize in, work as/with/in*.

**Tecnología (vocabulario académico de los textos):** *enhance, foster, overreliance,
withdrawal, reshape, multifaceted, raise concerns, lead to, striving, milestone, open-source,
integrate, automate, immersion, artificiality*.
